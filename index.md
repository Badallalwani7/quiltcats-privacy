# Privacy Policy for Quilt Cats

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Quilt Cats is a cozy cat logic puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

**The short version:** Quilt Cats has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. It doesn't collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

## Information the game stores on your device

To save your place, the game keeps the following **only on your device**:

- your level progress, stars and best times
- your daily puzzle streak and the cats you've unlocked
- your settings (sound, haptics, theme and similar)
- the puzzle you're in the middle of

This information is never uploaded. It is deleted when you use **Settings → Reset progress** or uninstall the game.

## Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Quilt Cats has no sign-up, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no servers of its own. The game works fully offline and doesn't request permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Sharing

We don't share any data with anyone, because we don't collect any.

## Children

Quilt Cats is designed for players of all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. It contains no ads, no chat and no links that leave the game.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Quilt Cats adds features that use data (for example, ads or in-app purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
