# Privacy Policy for Quilt Cats

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Quilt Cats is a cozy cat logic puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

**The short version:** Quilt Cats has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own, and your progress stays on your device. From version 1.2, the Android game is free with ads from Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play, processed with the help of RevenueCat. Those services receive some technical data, described below. Because the game is made for all ages, including children, its ads are never personalised and never based on what a player does in other apps.

## Information the game stores on your device

To save your place, the game keeps the following **only on your device**:

- your level progress, stars and best times
- your daily puzzle streak and the cats you've unlocked
- your hints and whether you bought "Remove ads"
- your settings (sound, haptics, theme and similar)
- the puzzle you're in the middle of

This information is never uploaded. It is deleted when you use **Settings → Reset progress** or uninstall the game.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

On Android, players can choose to watch a short video ad to earn hints. The game also shows an occasional full-screen ad between levels, never during the first 8 levels and never after "Remove ads" is bought.

Every ad request from Quilt Cats is marked as **child-directed** (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and Google Play's Families policy) and limited to content rated **G**. This means Google turns off personalised (interest-based) ads and remarketing for these requests. The game does not use the Android advertising ID: the permission that allows access to it is removed from the app.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends Google:

- the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location (approximate location, not GPS)
- ad and app interactions, such as app launches, ad views and taps
- diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance
- device identifiers such as the app set ID (not the advertising ID)

Google handles this data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

The Android game sells optional items: **Remove ads** and packs of **10 or 30 hints**. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your payment details. Google Play's parental controls let parents require approval for purchases.

To confirm purchases and restore "Remove ads" on a new device, the game uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). RevenueCat receives:

- purchase history: which products were bought, when, the price and the Google Play purchase token
- a random ID that the app creates (not linked to a name, email or account)
- the device's language and currency

Quilt Cats doesn't send RevenueCat any name, email address, location or advertising ID.

## Information we collect

We (the developer) don't collect personal data ourselves. Quilt Cats has no sign-up, no analytics or tracking tools, and no servers of its own. It doesn't request permissions such as location, contacts, camera or microphone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat receive, as described above. It is encrypted in transit.

## Sharing

We don't sell data. The only companies that receive data from the game are Google (ads and Google Play) and RevenueCat (purchases), for the purposes described above.

## Children

Quilt Cats is designed for players of all ages, including children. It has no chat, no sign-in and no way to share anything. Its ads are child-directed, rated G, never personalised and never use the advertising ID. An ad may open a store page or website if tapped. Watching ads is optional: a player only sees ads between levels, or chooses to watch one for hints.

Parents can ask us what purchase data RevenueCat holds for their child's device, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below.

## Your choices

- Watching an ad for hints is always optional.
- "Remove ads" stops the ads between levels.
- **Settings → Reset progress**, or uninstalling the game, deletes the data stored on your device.
- To have purchase records deleted from RevenueCat, email us with the approximate purchase date or the Google Play order number.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Quilt Cats changes how it uses data, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
